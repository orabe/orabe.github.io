About Me

I am a Computer Science Master's student at the Technical University of Berlin, specializing in the application of machine learning and advanced signal processing to biomedical data. My primary focus is on neuroimaging and understanding brain dynamics through computational methods.

Currently, as a Research Assistant at the Brain and Data Science Group at Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, I am contributing to the MNE-Python toolbox and developing well-calibrated inverse methods for M/EEG source modeling. My Master's thesis, supervised by Prof. Stefan Haufe, involves investigating subthalamic LFPs as a potential biomarker for adaptive deep brain stimulation in Parkinson's disease patients.

Beyond my research, I am passionate about education and community building. I founded and manage MathCodeLab, a teaching platform that provides courses in Mathematics and Computer Science to undergraduate students. I am also an active contributor to open-source scientific software.

Feel free to check out my CV, publications, and tutorials/courses, or contact me here.
