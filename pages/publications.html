Download Manuscript (2026) Project: GaitMod

JOSS Manuscript (In Preparation) GitHub Repository Project: CaliBrain

The paper documents the CaliBrain software stack, outlining its benchmarking datasets, uncertainty metrics, simulation engines, and visualization utilities for reproducible evaluation of source imaging algorithms.
