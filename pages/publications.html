Publications & Talks

A list of my academic publications, preprints, and conference presentations. My name is in bold. Links to related projects and software can be found on my CV page.

Ismail Huseynov, I., Orabe, M., Haufe, S., Nagarajan, S., & Hashemi, A. (In Preparation). Investigating the uncertainty estimation of inverse solvers in EEG/MEG brain source imaging.

Related Project: CaliBrain
Read more

This work investigates and benchmarks the performance of various Bayesian and frequentist inverse solvers for M/EEG source imaging, with a focus on the quality of their uncertainty estimates. The goal is to provide a framework for well-calibrated source reconstruction. Publication expected soon.

Orabe, M., & Haufe, S. (In Preparation). Detecting gait modulation with multimodal data (LFP, EEG, EMG, IMU) as a potential biomarker for clinical applications.

Related Project: GaitMod
Read more

This paper, based on my Master's thesis, details the development of a predictive biomarker from multimodal data for integration into implantable devices, aiming to enable frequency-based adaptive deep brain stimulation and improve patient outcomes.

Binns, T.S., Orabe, M., Nguyen, T.D., Köhler, R.M., Pellegrini, F., & Haufe, S. (2024). Multivariate connectivity methods in the MNE-Python toolbox. Neural Traces, Berlin, Germany. (Software Poster)

Related Project: MNE Contribution

This software poster was presented at the Neural Traces conference in Berlin, Germany, showcasing new multivariate connectivity features in the MNE-Python toolbox. View Poster
